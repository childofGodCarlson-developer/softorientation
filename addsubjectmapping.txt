Reuse the current architecture and design system.

The purpose of this development is to add a new major module:

# INTERNATIONAL CURRICULUM & SUBJECT EQUIVALENCY ENGINE

The module must solve a major international education problem:

Different countries organize academic knowledge differently.

A subject in Cameroon may contain several areas that are treated as separate subjects or courses in another country.

For example:

Cameroon:
Mathematics
├── Calculus
├── Algebra
├── Statistics
├── Geometry
└── other mathematical domains

A foreign university may separately require:

Calculus
Discrete Mathematics
Statistics
Linear Algebra

Therefore the application MUST NOT rely only on subject-name matching.

It must compare the CONTENT, TOPICS, SUBTOPICS, LEARNING OUTCOMES and PREREQUISITES contained inside subjects.

==================================================
8. MATCH TYPES
==============

Create these mapping statuses:

DIRECT_MATCH

The same or substantially equivalent subject exists.

EMBEDDED_MATCH

The foreign subject/topic exists inside a larger Cameroon subject.

DISTRIBUTED_MATCH

The foreign requirement is covered across multiple Cameroon subjects.

PARTIAL_MATCH

Only part of the required content has been identified.

RELATED_CONTENT

Related knowledge exists but does not satisfy the full requirement.

NO_MATCH_FOUND

No relevant curriculum content has been identified in the current knowledge base.

UNVERIFIED

Potential relationship identified but requires human verification.

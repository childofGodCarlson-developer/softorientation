==================================================
NORMALIZATION ENGINE
====================

For numeric grades, use:

normalized_percentage =
((student_score - minimum_score) /
(maximum_score - minimum_score)) * 100

For a standard 0–20 score:

normalized_percentage =
(student_score / 20) * 100

For a 0–100 score:

normalized_percentage =
student_score

For a 0–10 score:

normalized_percentage =
(student_score / 10) * 100

Always round display values appropriately but retain the raw calculation internally.
